Meta snapshot bracket: Divine 25,773 ranked matches OpenDota public data

Dota 2 Meta

Aggregated from public matches, not opinion. Win rates are shrinkage-adjusted so a hot 40-game hero never outranks a proven 40,000-game one.

Tier list

Ranked by adjusted win rate. Bar spans 45–57%; the hairline is 50%. Pick share is what fraction of games the hero appears in.

Hardest counters

Head-to-head win rate when both heroes are in the game on opposite teams, centred on 50% and shrunk toward zero. Only meta-tier heroes shown. Small samples — directional, not final.

Best synergy pairs

Pair win rate minus what the two heroes do individually (combined on the log-odds scale). Positive = the pair wins more than the sum of its parts. Preliminary at this sample size.

How to read it
Tier score
(wins + k/2) / (games + k), k ≈ 250 — pulls low-sample heroes toward 50%.
Counters
From OpenDota /matchups. adv(A,B) = winRate(A vs B) − 0.5, shrunk.
Synergy
Same-team pair win rate minus an independent-contribution baseline from each hero's solo rate.
Bracket
OpenDota rank bracket. Divine ≈ top ~4% of ranked. Immortal isn't split out by the API.
Freshness
Rolling window, a few days to a patch. Re-run scripts/fetch.py to refresh; use --matches 200000 for firm pair stats.
